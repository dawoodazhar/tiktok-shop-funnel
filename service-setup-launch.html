Setup & Launch

For brands not yet live on TikTok Shop

What's Included

  • Shop configuration and catalog upload
  • TikTok Pixel and event tracking installation (ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, CompletePayment, Purchase)
  • Verification of every event in TikTok's Event Manager before any campaign launches
  • Initial campaign structure ready for launch on clean data

Timeline

Shop and pixel setup typically happens within days. Confirming any market-specific requirements (like German Impressum/VAT groundwork) can extend this, and is scoped up front during your audit.

Pricing

Setup & Launch is quoted per shop based on catalog size and market requirements, not a flat rate. Most sellers get an exact number the same day they book a free TikTok Shop growth audit.

FAQ

How much does TikTok Shop setup cost?
Pricing is scoped per shop during a free audit, based on catalog size and whether German VAT/Impressum groundwork is needed.

How long does TikTok Shop setup take?
Typically a few days once catalog data is ready; market-specific legal or VAT requirements can extend this and are scoped up front.

Do I need a TikTok Pixel before running ads?
Yes. Every event is verified individually in TikTok's Event Manager before any campaign launches, since GMV Max depends entirely on clean pixel data.
