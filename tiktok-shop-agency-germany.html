TikTok Shop Agency for Germany

GMV Forge is a TikTok Shop growth agency based in Offenbach am Main, near Frankfurt. We set up, run and scale TikTok Shops for e-commerce brands selling to German and Austrian customers: shop setup and tracking, GMV Max ads, and creator affiliate programs, managed by one strategist you talk to directly.

Why TikTok Shop in Germany, and why now

TikTok Shop opened to German sellers on March 31, 2025. In June 2026 it expanded to Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland, and added "Sell Across Europe", which lets one seller registration reach several EU markets. Since January 8, 2026 the standard seller fee in Germany is 9% of the sale (7% for electronics), with a reduced 4% rate for new sellers during their first 60 days if they meet TikTok's conditions.

That combination makes the next year unusual: the channel is live and growing, the fee structure is now clear enough to plan margins, and most German brands still have no one on their team who has run GMV Max or an affiliate program at scale.

What is different about selling in Germany

The platform mechanics are the same as in the UK or US: catalog, tracking, content, GMV Max. What changes is everything around them:

  • Seller requirements. You need a TikTok business account and must register in Seller Center with a business registration and proof of identity. There is no minimum follower count for businesses in Germany.
  • Pricing rules. Prices must be shown as total prices including VAT and all fees, in line with German price display rules.
  • Margins. A 9% seller fee plus creator commission plus ad spend has to fit inside your margin. Our TikTok Shop Germany fee calculator shows the math per unit.
  • Content. German shoppers respond to native, information-forward videos. Machine-translated US creative and listings tend to underperform.
  • Fulfilment and returns. Shipping times and return handling are watched closely. "Fulfilled by TikTok" is available as an alternative to shipping yourself.

The full breakdown is in our TikTok Shop Germany guide.

FAQ

Is TikTok Shop available in Germany?

Yes. TikTok Shop launched in Germany on March 31, 2025, together with France and Italy. Austria followed on June 15, 2026.

What does a TikTok Shop agency in Germany do?

It sets up the shop in Seller Center, connects tracking and the catalog, runs GMV Max campaigns, recruits and manages affiliate creators, and reports on GMV, orders and ad spend. GMV Forge does all of this from Offenbach am Main.

How much does GMV Forge cost for a German shop?

GMV Max Ads Management is $1,000 per month, Full Shop Management is $1,500 per month, and Creator & Influencer Outreach is $1,000 per month, each covering up to 2 products. Setup and larger scopes are quoted after a free audit. Ad spend is paid separately to TikTok.

We prepare the German-market setup checklist (legal notice page, VAT registration and invoicing setup) as part of Setup & Launch, and coordinate with your tax advisor where tax or legal questions arise. GMV Forge does not provide legal or tax advice.
