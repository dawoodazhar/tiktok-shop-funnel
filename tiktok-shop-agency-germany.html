Anologe is based in Germany and works with e-commerce brands selling into the German TikTok Shop market, alongside clients in the US and UK.

What's Different About the German Market

The core mechanics of TikTok Shop are the same everywhere: catalog, pixel, content, GMV Max. What changes for Germany is everything around it — legal notice (Impressum) requirements, VAT registration and invoicing, and content that is genuinely localized rather than machine-translated. We cover this in detail in our TikTok Shop Germany guide.
