TikTok agency vs TikTok Shop agency

A general TikTok agency often focuses on brand awareness: organic videos, influencer campaigns and views. A TikTok Shop agency is judged on sales inside TikTok. GMV Forge works the second way: every video, creator and ad is tracked back to orders, GMV and ad spend.

  • Goal: orders and profitable GMV, not only views or followers.
  • Ads: GMV Max and shop ads tied to your product catalogue.
  • Creators: affiliates paid on commission, so cost follows sales.

How to choose a TikTok agency

  1. Ask how they measure success. Look for GMV, orders and return on ad spend, not only reach.
  2. Check tracking first. A good agency audits your pixel and Events API before spending on ads.
  3. Get clear pricing. Know the retainer, any commission, and what is included per product.
  4. Check who owns the accounts. Your Seller Center, ads account and creator contacts should stay yours.
  5. Look for proof. Ask for case studies with real numbers and verified reviews.
