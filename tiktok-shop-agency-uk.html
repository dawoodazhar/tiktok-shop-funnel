TikTok Shop UK fees in 2026

  • Commission: TikTok's UK Seller Center guidance sets the standard commission at 9% of the order amount, inclusive of VAT. Selected electronics and beauty subcategories can have lower rates; check your category in Seller Center.
  • No listing or monthly fee: opening a UK shop and listing products is free.
  • Creator commission: set by you, separate from the platform fee; 10–20% of the product price is common.
  • Ads and fulfilment: GMV Max spend, samples and shipping are on top, and together they usually cost more than the 9% commission.

Work backwards from your price: price minus VAT, 9% commission, creator commission, shipping and product cost. What is left is your maximum ad spend per order. Our fee calculator does the same maths (set the fee to 9% and VAT to 20%).

What is different about selling on TikTok Shop UK

  • The most mature TikTok Shop market in Europe: UK shoppers are used to buying from creator videos and LIVEs, so competition for creators and ad inventory is higher than in Germany.
  • VAT at 20%: prices are shown including VAT, so your margin maths starts from the net price.
  • Delivery expectations: fast dispatch matters for your shop score; set handling times you can actually meet, or use Fulfilled by TikTok for best-sellers.
  • UK to EU: a UK shop does not automatically sell into Germany or Austria. EU markets need their own setup (see TikTok Shop agency Europe).
