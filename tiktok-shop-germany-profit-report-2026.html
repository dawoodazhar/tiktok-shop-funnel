TikTok Shop Germany Profit Report 2026: What Sellers Keep per Order

By Dawood Azhar · Published Oct 16, 2026

Key finding: after TikTok Shop's 9% seller fee, 19% VAT, creator commission, shipping and product cost, a typical German seller keeps between €3 and €9 per order before advertising, or 15% to 36% of net revenue, in our eight modelled categories. That amount is the most a seller can spend on ads per order without losing money. The fee rise from 5% to 9% on January 8, 2026 removed about €0.80 to €2.00 of that buffer per order.

What this means for sellers

  • Low-price products are squeezed hardest. Under about €20, fixed costs like shipping take a large share, leaving around €4 per order for ads.
  • High-margin categories still work well. Supplements and beauty keep the most room for GMV Max and creator commissions.
  • Bulky or low-margin goods need bundles. Pet and fitness products need higher order values or cheaper shipping to fund ads.
  • Set GMV Max ROI targets from this number. If €5 is left per order on a €25 sale, the campaign must return at least 5x to break even.

Methodology

This is a modelled scenario, not client data. Inputs: TikTok Shop Germany seller fee of 9% (7% for electronics) since January 8, 2026, previously 5%; German VAT 19%; a creator commission of 15% applied to half of orders; typical shipping and packaging costs; and product cost as a share of price typical for each category (22% to 40%). Your numbers will differ; run your own with the TikTok Shop Germany fee calculator (Deutsch).
