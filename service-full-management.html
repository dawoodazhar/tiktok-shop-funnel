Full TikTok Shop Management

For brands stalled at $5–30K/month who want one team to run it all

Full TikTok Shop management means GMV Forge runs your shop, your GMV Max ads and your content as one coordinated account. One strategist owns the numbers, so the products in your ads, your shop tab and your videos point in the same direction.

Who it's for

  • Brands already selling on TikTok Shop whose growth has stalled, often around $5,000–30,000 a month.
  • Teams where ads, listings and content are handled by different people who rarely coordinate.
  • Founders who want one accountable person instead of several freelancers.

What we do

  • Everything in GMV Max Ads Management: campaigns, ROI targets, budgets, creative rotation.
  • Shop tab setup and ongoing maintenance.
  • Listing optimization: titles, descriptions and images.
  • Inventory and order monitoring.
  • Shoppable content posting and scheduling.
  • Weekly and monthly reporting.

How it works

  • Week 1: audit tracking, catalog and performance; fix tracking gaps.
  • Week 2: launch or restructure GMV Max on clean data.
  • Weeks 3–4: weekly content cadence and campaign tuning.
  • Ongoing: scale what converts, weekly reports.

Proof
