TikTok Shop Germany: What Brands Need to Know (2026)

By Dawood Azhar · Published Aug 12, 2026 · Updated Oct 10, 2026 · All guides

Short answer: TikTok Shop has been live in Germany since March 31, 2025. Sellers pay a 9% fee per sale (7% for electronics) since January 8, 2026, need a registered business and a TikTok business account, and must show VAT-inclusive prices. Austria joined on June 15, 2026.

This guide is general information, not legal or tax advice. Confirm your obligations with a German tax advisor or lawyer before launching.

When did TikTok Shop launch in Germany?

TikTok Shop opened in Germany, France and Italy on March 31, 2025, after earlier launches in the UK, Spain and Ireland. Brands such as About You, NIVEA and essence were among the first German sellers. On June 15, 2026 TikTok added Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland, bringing TikTok Shop to ten European countries, and introduced "Sell Across Europe" so one seller registration can cover several EU markets.

How much does TikTok Shop charge sellers in Germany?

From January 8, 2026, the seller fee in Germany, Spain, France, Italy and Ireland rose from 5% to 9% per sale. Electronics are capped at 7%. Sellers who join after that date can get a 4% rate for their first 60 days if they meet the conditions, including uploading at least five products within 15 days. There is no monthly fee; you pay per item sold.

On top of the seller fee, budget for creator commissions on affiliate sales, ad spend, shipping and returns. Use our TikTok Shop Germany fee calculator to check your margin per unit before you launch.

What do you need to start selling?

  • A TikTok business account; a personal account is not enough.
  • Registration in TikTok Seller Center as a company or sole trader, with documents such as your business registration and proof of identity.
  • Allowed products. TikTok Shop has a list of prohibited and restricted items; check it in the German Seller Academy before building your catalog.
  • No follower minimum for businesses opening a shop in Germany.

What about VAT and the legal notice?

Selling to German consumers means acting as a business: you need to register the business, issue invoices and, depending on turnover and where you are based, charge and report VAT. Prices on TikTok Shop must be shown as total prices including VAT and fees. A German-market storefront is also generally expected to have a legal notice page identifying the business. Non-EU sellers should confirm their VAT position (including whether EU One-Stop-Shop reporting applies) with an advisor before listing products.

How do shipping, payment and returns work?

Customers pay through TikTok, and the seller is paid out after successful delivery. Sellers handle shipping themselves, or can use "Fulfilled by TikTok", which works similarly to Amazon FBA. German shoppers pay close attention to delivery times and returns, so set realistic shipping promises before you spend on ads.

How do creators and affiliates work in Germany?

Creators can promote other brands' products through the affiliate program and earn a commission on each sale, in videos and LIVE streams. For most brands, affiliates are the fastest way to build content volume: dozens of creators posting about a product gives GMV Max far more material to work with than a brand account posting alone. Our affiliate and creator management guide covers commission strategy and outreach.

How should German brands use GMV Max?

GMV Max is TikTok's automated campaign type for TikTok Shop that optimizes paid and organic traffic for total GMV. In Germany the mechanics are the same as elsewhere; what matters is having clean tracking, a localized catalog, and enough German-language videos linked to your products. TikTok recommends starting from an ROI target based on your historical GMV and ad cost and leaving each ROI setting for at least three full days before changing it. See our GMV Max guide and GMV Max troubleshooting guide.

What content works for German shoppers?

Native, entertaining videos still win, but German audiences tend to respond better to content that explains the product clearly and sounds less like an ad. Write listings in German rather than machine-translating US or UK copy, and brief creators in German.

A realistic launch timeline

  • Days 1–7: business account, Seller Center registration, document verification, catalog in German, tracking set up and verified.
  • Weeks 2–3: first affiliate outreach and samples, brand videos posted, GMV Max launched on clean data.
  • Weeks 4+: scale the products and creators that convert, and keep the content supply steady.

The paperwork and localization usually take longer than the technical setup. Brands that prepare them first see steadier early results than brands that launch with untranslated US or UK assets.

TikTok Shop Germany seller checklist

Work through this list before your first GMV Max campaign. It is general information, not legal or tax advice.

  1. Business: registered business (company or sole trader) and proof of identity ready for verification.
  2. Accounts: TikTok business account and a TikTok Seller Center account for Germany.
  3. Products: catalog checked against TikTok Shop's prohibited and restricted items list.
  4. Legal notice: a legal notice page for your German-market storefront.
  5. VAT and invoicing: VAT position confirmed with your tax advisor (including OSS if you sell cross-border); invoices set up.
  6. Prices: shown as total prices including VAT and all fees.
  7. Margin: unit economics checked with the fee calculator (9% seller fee, creator commission, ads).
  8. Listings: titles and descriptions written in German, not machine-translated.
  9. Fulfilment and returns: shipping promise and returns process set, or Fulfilled by TikTok chosen.
  10. Tracking: TikTok Pixel and events verified in Event Manager (pixel guide).
  11. Content: first German-language videos posted and creator affiliate outreach started.
  12. Launch: GMV Max started with a realistic ROI target (troubleshooting guide).

What about Austria and the rest of Europe?

Austria has been live since June 15, 2026. With "Sell Across Europe", a German-language catalog and creator program can serve Austrian shoppers as well. Read more on our TikTok Shop Europe page.

FAQ

When did TikTok Shop launch in Germany?

March 31, 2025, together with France and Italy.

What are TikTok Shop seller fees in Germany?

Since January 8, 2026 the standard seller fee is 9% per sale, 7% for electronics. New sellers can get a reduced 4% rate for their first 60 days if they meet TikTok's conditions, such as uploading at least 5 products within 15 days. There is no monthly account fee.

What do I need to sell on TikTok Shop in Germany?

A TikTok business account, a registered business (for example a business registration certificate), proof of identity, and products that are allowed under TikTok Shop policies. You register in TikTok Seller Center.

Is TikTok Shop available in Austria?

Yes, since June 15, 2026, alongside Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland.

Do creators need followers to sell as affiliates?

Creator programs can have minimum requirements, often around 1,000 followers depending on the program. Businesses opening a shop have no minimum follower requirement in Germany.
