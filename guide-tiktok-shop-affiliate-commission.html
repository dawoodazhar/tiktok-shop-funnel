TikTok Shop Affiliate Commission: What Rate Should Sellers Offer?

By GMV Forge Team · Published Nov 10, 2026 · All guides

Short answer: You set the commission yourself, per product. Published 2026 benchmarks put most US programs between 5% and 30%, with 15% to 20% common, and beauty and supplements higher. The right rate is the one your margin can pay on every affiliate order, not the highest one in your category.

How Commission Works

Commission is the share of the sale price you pay a creator when their video, LIVE or showcase link produces an order. You pay it only on those orders. It is separate from TikTok's referral fee, so an affiliate order carries both. TikTok pays the creator; you never handle the payout.

Typical Commission Rates by Category

These benchmarks were published by Hubfluence, a TikTok Shop affiliate software company, for US programs in 2026. They are that company's observations, not official TikTok figures.

CategoryTypical range
Beauty and personal care15 to 25%
Health and supplements20 to 30%
Home and kitchen10 to 20%
Fashion and apparel10 to 15%
Electronics and gadgets5 to 15%
Food and beverage10 to 20%

Source: Hubfluence, "TikTok Shop Commission: Rates & Benchmarks 2026".

Open Plan or Targeted Plan?

  • Open plan: any eligible creator can add your product and earn the rate you set. Good for reach.
  • Targeted plan: you invite specific creators and can offer them a different, usually higher, rate. Good for keeping creators who already sell for you.

A common setup is an open plan at about the category average and a targeted plan a few points higher for your best creators.

Find Your Maximum Rate in 4 Steps

  1. Start with your selling price.
  2. Subtract the referral fee, product cost, shipping and packaging.
  3. Subtract the profit you need per order.
  4. What is left, as a percentage of the price, is the highest commission you can offer.

Example (not client data): price $30, referral fee $1.80 (6%), product $8, shipping $5, target profit $6. That leaves $9.20, so the ceiling is about 30%. At a 15% commission ($4.50) you keep $10.70 before ads. The referral fee differs by category, so use the rate shown in your own Seller Center.

Run your own numbers in the TikTok Shop fees calculator, which has a field for creator commission.

When to Change the Rate

  • Raise it if creators in your category are offered more elsewhere and few are picking your product.
  • Lower it once a product sells steadily through many creators, and move that margin to ads or targeted plans.
  • A new rate applies to future sales; orders already attributed keep the old rate.

Why Creators Still Say No

A fair rate is not enough. Creators also look at the price point, reviews, whether samples are free and fast, and whether the product is easy to show on camera. If outreach gets no replies, check those before raising commission. See how affiliate management works and what creator outreach costs.

Affiliate Commission FAQ

What is a good TikTok Shop commission rate?

Most published benchmarks sit between 10% and 20%, higher in beauty and supplements. Start near your category's range and check it against your margin.

Who sets the commission on TikTok Shop?

The seller sets it, per product, in Seller Center.

Do I pay commission on every order?

No. You pay commission only on orders attributed to a creator's video, LIVE or showcase link.

Is commission the same as the referral fee?

No. Commission goes to the creator. The referral fee goes to TikTok. An affiliate order carries both.
