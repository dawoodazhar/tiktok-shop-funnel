How to set a GMV Max ROI target

Your break-even ROI is the selling price divided by the most you can spend on ads per order. Example for one product:

ItemAmount
Selling price$30.00
Product cost + shipping−$12.00
TikTok referral fee (8%)−$2.40
Left for ads and profit$15.60
Break-even ROI$30 ÷ $15.60 ≈ 1.9

A target below 1.9 would lose money on every order; a target well above it may stop the campaign from spending. Work out your own numbers with the TikTok Shop fees calculator. If a campaign stalls, see GMV Max not spending: 7 fixes.

  • Hire an agency if spend is growing, ROI swings from day to day, or nobody owns tracking and creative testing.

FAQ

What does a TikTok ads agency do?

It plans, launches and optimises paid TikTok campaigns. For TikTok Shop sellers that mainly means GMV Max: choosing products, setting ROI targets from your margins, feeding new videos into the campaign and checking that purchase tracking is correct.

How much does a TikTok ads agency cost?

GMV Forge charges a flat $1,000 per month for GMV Max ads management for up to 2 products. Your ad spend is paid directly to TikTok and is separate. Many agencies charge a retainer plus a percentage of ad spend instead.

What is a good ROI target for GMV Max?

Start from your break-even ROI: selling price divided by the most you can spend on ads per order after product cost, TikTok fees, creator commission and shipping. Set the target near or above that number and adjust after the first days of data.

Do you run TikTok ads for brands without TikTok Shop?

Our focus is TikTok Shop sellers, where ads, creators and checkout sit inside TikTok and results can be measured as orders and GMV.
