FAQ

How much does a TikTok Shop agency cost in the US?

GMV Forge charges flat monthly prices: $1,000 for GMV Max Ads Management, $1,000 for Creator & Influencer Outreach and $1,500 for Full Shop Management, each for up to 2 products. Ad spend and creator commissions are paid separately to TikTok.

Do you take a percentage of sales?

No. The fee is a fixed monthly price. There is no percentage of sales.

Is GMV Forge an official TikTok Shop Partner?

No. GMV Forge is an independent agency, not an official TikTok Shop Partner.

Can an agency in Germany manage a US TikTok Shop?

Yes. Seller Center and TikTok Ads Manager are managed online with access you grant and can remove at any time. Products ship from your US warehouse or 3PL as before.
