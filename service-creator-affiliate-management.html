Creator & Affiliate Management

For shops that want creator content and affiliate sales

Creator and affiliate management means building a steady group of TikTok creators who post about your products and earn a commission on the sales they drive. GMV Forge finds and contacts creators, handles samples and commission rates, collects their videos, and tracks which ones actually sell.

Who it's for

  • Brands live on TikTok Shop with few or no creators posting about them.
  • Shops whose affiliate program stalled after a handful of one-off posts.
  • Brands running GMV Max that need more product-linked videos.

What we do

  • Creator research: creators whose audience and content style match your category and market.
  • Outreach and onboarding: invitations, follow-ups and clear briefs.
  • Samples: coordinating sample requests and shipping arrangements.
  • Commission strategy: setting and negotiating rates that attract creators without breaking your margin.
  • Content collection: gathering creator videos so the best ones can be reused in GMV Max and Spark Ads.
  • Performance tracking: which creators post, which convert, and who deserves a higher commission or a repeat collaboration.

How it works

  • Weeks 1–2: commission plan, creator shortlist, first invitations and samples.
  • Weeks 3–4: first videos live, early sales data, follow-ups with creators who haven't posted.
  • Ongoing: keep recruiting, reward creators who convert, and feed the best videos into ads.

What you receive

  • Managed creator outreach for up to 2 products.
  • A record of creators contacted, sampled and posting.
  • A monthly creator performance report.

Proof

In our $398K GMV case study, the shop tab, GMV Max spend and organic content were run as one system: GMV Max was pointed at the organic videos already converting, with 3–4 shoppable videos a week. Creator content works the same way, as fuel for both organic sales and ads. Founder Dawood Azhar has also run creator and affiliate outreach programs for Germany-based brands.

Pricing

$1,000 per month for up to 2 products, standalone or as an add-on to GMV Max Management or Full Shop Management. All pricing

FAQ

What does TikTok Shop affiliate management include?

Finding suitable creators, contacting them, arranging product samples, setting commission rates, collecting the videos they post, and tracking which creators drive sales.

How much does creator and affiliate management cost?

$1,000 per month for up to 2 products, either standalone or added to GMV Max or Full Shop Management. Creator commissions are paid through TikTok Shop and are separate.

Who pays the creators?

Affiliate creators earn a commission on the sales they generate, paid through TikTok Shop's affiliate program. Samples are a product cost for the brand.

Can affiliate videos be used in ads?

Yes. Affiliate videos can be used in GMV Max, and TikTok's own guidance notes they often perform well there.
