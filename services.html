FAQ

How much does a TikTok Shop agency cost?
GMV Forge charges flat monthly rates: GMV Max Ads Management is $1,000 per month, Full Shop Management $1,500 per month, and Creator & Influencer Outreach $1,000 per month, each for up to 2 products. Setup, more products or combined services are quoted on a free audit call. Ad spend is paid separately to TikTok. Full pricing.

Which service is right for my brand?
Not yet live: Setup & Launch. Already live, ready to scale ads: GMV Max Management. Want creator-driven organic sales: Creator & Affiliate Management. Stalled at $5-30K/month: Full Management.

Can I switch services as my shop grows?
Yes. Most brands start with Setup & Launch or GMV Max Management, then move into Full Management once they need everything coordinated together.
