TikTok Shop Partner (TSP) vs. Agency: What Brands Should Know

By Dawood Azhar · Updated Oct 10, 2026

Short answer: a TikTok Shop Partner (TSP) is a service provider that TikTok has reviewed and approved, listed in its partner directory. A TikTok Shop agency is any company that runs TikTok Shop for brands. Many agencies are TSPs, many are not. Partner status tells you TikTok vetted the company; it does not tell you whether the people on your account can grow your shop.

What is a TikTok Shop Partner (TSP)?

TSPs apply through the TikTok Shop Partner Center, are reviewed, and once approved can be found by sellers in the partner directory and in Seller Center. Approved partners get partner resources and, at higher GMV tiers, dedicated TikTok support. TikTok runs related programs for different roles:

  • TSP (TikTok Shop Partner): service partners for shop operations, content, creators and paid media.
  • Creator Agency Partners (MCN/CAP): agencies that manage creators.
  • Affiliate Partners (TAP): partners that run affiliate campaigns for sellers.
  • App developers (ISVs): software that connects to TikTok Shop, not services.

Applicants typically need a registered business and proof that they can deliver the service. Requirements differ by region, so check Partner Center for current terms.

How to check any TikTok Shop agency

  1. Ask for Seller Center screenshots of GMV and orders for a comparable brand.
  2. Ask how they check tracking (TikTok Pixel and Events API) before spending budget.
  3. Ask how they recruit creators who sell, not just creators with followers.
  4. Confirm the fee, the contract length and what reporting you get.
  5. Start with a 2–3 month trial if you can.

Frequently asked questions

What is a TikTok Shop Partner?

A TikTok Shop Partner (TSP) is a service provider that TikTok has reviewed and approved through Partner Center. Sellers can find TSPs in the partner directory and Seller Center.

Is a TSP better than a regular agency?

Not automatically. Partner status means TikTok vetted the company and may provide support at higher GMV tiers. Results in Seller Center, who runs your account and pricing matter more.

How does an agency become a TikTok Shop Partner?

It applies in TikTok Shop Partner Center, chooses a market and business category, and submits business documents for review. Requirements vary by region.
