About GMV Forge

TikTok Shop growth agency · Offenbach am Main, Germany

GMV Forge is a TikTok Shop growth and management agency based in Offenbach am Main, Germany. A small team of 3–5 specialists sets up, runs and scales TikTok Shop for e-commerce brands in Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States: shop setup and tracking, GMV Max campaigns, creator affiliate programs and content.

The team

We keep the team small on purpose, so the people who plan your account are the people who run it.

  • Strategy and account lead. Owns your growth plan, targets and reporting, and is your main contact.
  • Paid media. Builds and optimises GMV Max, Spark Ads and product campaigns.
  • Creator partnerships. Recruits and manages affiliate creators, samples and commissions.
  • Content and shop operations. Product listings, catalog data, short-form video briefs and shop health.

Why GMV Forge exists

Most brands that struggle on TikTok Shop don't need another dashboard. They need a team that can see the problem in the data and fix it quickly. GMV Forge is built around that: a small senior team, one lead strategist per account, and no rotating account managers.

How we work

  • Data first. Every pixel event is verified in Event Manager before budget goes up.
  • One system. Shop tab, GMV Max and content are planned together, not in separate silos.
  • Real numbers. Results are reported from TikTok Shop Seller Center and Ads Manager.
  • One main contact. Each client has a lead strategist, backed by the specialists running the account.

Results

  • More than $440,000 in TikTok Shop GMV managed.
  • $398,812 GMV and 9,785 orders in a single 30-day engagement.
  • 9.35× ROI on a 7-day GMV Max campaign.
