Dawood Azhar

TikTok Shop & Performance Marketing Specialist

Background

Dawood is based in Frankfurt/Offenbach, Germany, and has run TikTok Shop, Meta Ads, and Google Ads campaigns for e-commerce clients since late 2024.

His technical marketing background spans TikTok Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Shop operations, and marketing automation tooling, giving him both the strategic and technical grounding to plan campaigns and troubleshoot pixel or catalog issues directly, rather than relying on templated agency playbooks.

What Dawood Does at Anologe

Dawood plans the strategy behind every Anologe account: shop and pixel setup, GMV Max campaign structure, product discoverability, and content direction. He works directly with clients rather than through a rotating account-manager layer.

A small execution team supports day-to-day production work, including content edits, reporting, and campaign monitoring, but the strategy and client relationship stay with Dawood.

Results

Engagements led by Dawood have managed over $440,000 in TikTok Shop GMV across active accounts, including a single 30-day engagement that generated $398,812 in GMV across 9,785 orders, and a separate campaign returning 9.35× ROI on TikTok GMV Max ad spend.
