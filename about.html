About

Dawood Azhar

Founder, GMV Forge · Offenbach am Main, Germany

Dawood Azhar is the founder of GMV Forge, a TikTok Shop growth and management agency based in Offenbach am Main, Germany. He plans and runs the strategy behind every client account: shop setup and tracking, GMV Max campaigns, and creator affiliate programs.

Background

Dawood started as an iOS developer and has four apps on the App Store. He moved into performance marketing deliberately, because the problems that hold back e-commerce ads are usually data problems: broken tracking, mismatched catalog data, events that fire but report the wrong value. Before GMV Forge he ran his own Shopify store with Meta Ads, handling everything from product listings to fulfilment, and worked as a performance marketing consultant for a US-based company. He has run TikTok Shop campaigns since 2024, alongside Meta and Google Ads for e-commerce brands, and holds the HubSpot Academy Digital Marketing certification.

Why GMV Forge exists

Most brands that struggle on TikTok Shop don't need another dashboard. They need one person who can see the problem in the data and fix it the same day. GMV Forge is built around that: one strategist, direct access, and no rotating account managers.

How we work

  • Data first. Every pixel event is verified in Event Manager before budget goes up.
  • One system. Shop tab, GMV Max and content are planned together, not in separate silos.
  • Real numbers. Results are reported from TikTok Shop Seller Center and Ads Manager.
  • Direct access. Clients work with Dawood, not a junior account manager.

Results

  • More than $440,000 in TikTok Shop GMV managed.
  • $398,812 GMV and 9,785 orders in a single 30-day engagement.
  • 9.35× ROI on a 7-day GMV Max campaign.
