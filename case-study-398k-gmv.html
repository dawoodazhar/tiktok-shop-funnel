The Problem

The shop had a live TikTok Shop presence but was underperforming relative to its product catalog: shop tab listings weren't optimized for discoverability, paid and organic content weren't coordinated, and there was no structured approach to feeding new content into ad campaigns as older creative fatigued. Sales were happening, but inconsistently, with no clear system driving repeatable volume.

The Approach

Anologe took over shop tab optimization, paid marketing, and organic content strategy as one coordinated effort rather than three separate workstreams. Product listings were restructured for discoverability within TikTok's shop tab and search surfaces. Paid GMV Max spend was aligned with the organic content pipeline so ad budget could pull from proven organic performers rather than starting cold on every new creative. A shoppable video content cadence was established to keep the creative pool fresh, since stale creative is one of the most common reasons GMV Max performance plateaus over a multi-week engagement.

Growth was driven by improvements across all three sales channels working together, shop tab, organic, and paid, rather than any single lever.

The Numbers

Over 30 days, the shop generated $398,812 in total GMV across 9,785 orders and 24,549 items sold, averaging 306 daily customers. This represented a substantial step up from the shop's prior baseline and established a repeatable operating pattern rather than a one-off spike, the account's clean pixel and catalog data (see our Pixel Setup Guide) meant performance held steady rather than reverting once the initial optimization push ended.

What Made This Repeatable

The result wasn't driven by a single viral moment. It came from treating shop tab optimization, paid, and organic content as one system rather than separate deliverables, and from having clean underlying data so that GMV Max ad spend was optimizing against accurate signal from the start.
